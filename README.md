# FREDDY: From Physical to Digital

## CIM423 Building Virtual Worlds

**Platform:** MUD Foundation

## Project Overview

FREDDY: From Physical to Digital is an immersive virtual sculpture experience built in MUD Foundation. The project began with a real handmade ceramic sculpture that was 3D scanned and brought into a shared virtual environment.

The experience explores how the perception of a physical artwork changes when it is no longer restricted by its original scale. Visitors first encounter Freddy at approximately its real-world size before moving toward a monumental version of the same sculpture. A spiral staircase allows visitors to travel around the enlarged sculpture and observe it from viewpoints that would be impossible in a traditional physical exhibition.

Interactive points throughout the experience reveal additional information about the sculpture and its creation process. A final process gallery documents the transition from physical sculpture to 3D scan to immersive virtual object.

## Experience Structure

### Zone 1 — The Physical Object
Visitors encounter Freddy at approximately its real-world scale alongside photographs, physical dimensions, material information, and an interactive Learn More display.

### Zone 2 — Beyond Physical Scale
A monumental version of Freddy becomes the centerpiece of the experience. Visitors can walk or teleport around a spiral staircase surrounding the sculpture and interact with informational hotspots.

### Zone 3 — From Physical to Digital
A process gallery presents photographs and information documenting the original sculpture, the 3D scanning process, and its transformation inside MUD Foundation.
